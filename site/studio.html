Studio

We believe that structure is both a foundation and a reflection of identity.

btl is short for between the lines. The practice works from a fourth-floor room on Mavoor Road in Kozhikode, and takes on houses, interiors and the occasional commercial project across Kerala.

The work tends to begin with what is already there — an old tree, a reclaimed door, the earth dug out to make a pond. Our dedication to precision, reliability and quiet confidence defines every project we undertake.
